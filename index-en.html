Case 02

Agricultural productivity

From static averages to a prescriptive harvest plan

Context

Blueberry agroindustry with guesswork planning, surprise overtime, and bullwhip effect.

Question

How to turn historical records into a weekly plan for hours, gaps, and costs with uncertainty visible?

Finding

Averages hid a vicious cycle: learning curve impact, fatigue, seasonality, and labor competition.

Decision

A zoo of forecasts for a prescriptive model: hire when break-even justifies it, recalibrate targets in stress windows.

Case 03

Exchange rate sensitivity

What hurts the most, in one number

Context

Colombian rose exporter.

Challenge

Operations depend on the dollar, wages, waste, price, and freight. Every movement changes the margin, with no tool to quantify it.

Method

Sensitivity model translating each variable's movement into pesos of profit lost or gained. Tornado diagram to prioritize management levers.

Result

Each sensitivity points to a different lever. The model quantifies exposure; the client makes the decision.
